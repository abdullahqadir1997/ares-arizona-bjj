Common Questions

Everything you need to know before stepping on the mats.

Still have questions? Give us a call at (480) 491-5440 — we're happy to talk you through it.

How do I enroll?
The first step is to book your free trial class right here on our website. Once you've had your class, we'll walk you through every membership option and find the right fit for your goals, schedule, and budget.
Do I need any prior training experience?
No — most of our students walk in with zero prior experience. We have classes built specifically for complete beginners. Book a free class and come try us out.
Do I need to be in shape before starting?
Not at all. Starting Jiu-Jitsu now will help you get in shape and reach your fitness goals. Train at your own pace — your conditioning will follow.
What is the training environment like?
We run a friendly, family-style training environment that welcomes everyone — from competitive athletes to weekend hobbyists, from kids to adults to entire families.
What should I wear to my first class?
Wear comfortable workout clothing, easily removable shoes, and bring a water bottle. We'll provide everything else you need — including a Gi if you don't have one yet.
