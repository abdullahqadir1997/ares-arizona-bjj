What Happens Next

A few simple next steps.

  1. 01
    Check your inbox & phone You'll get a confirmation email and SMS within minutes. We'll send a reminder the day before your class.
  2. 02
    Show up 10–15 min early Park on the south side of the building. Walk in, sign the waiver at the front, and a coach will meet you on the mat.
  3. 03
    Wear comfy clothes & bring water Athletic shorts and a t-shirt are perfect. We'll loan you a gi for the class — no need to buy anything.
  4. 04
    Have fun. No pressure. You'll learn a few moves, drill them with a partner, and leave with a smile. That's the whole goal of day one.
