Gilbert Rd to US-60 west is the fastest approach; Southern Ave is a close second if traffic is heavy on the freeway.

Gilbert FAQs

What Gilbert residents ask us.

How far is Ares Arizona from Gilbert?
About 9.6 miles — roughly 16 minutes via US-60 or Southern Ave. From south Gilbert (Higley/Power Ranch), plan for ~22 minutes.
Do you have a Kids Jiu-Jitsu program suitable for Gilbert families?
Yes — three age-graded Kids classes daily (Little Warriors 4-6, Juniors 7-10, Pre-Teens 11-14). Many of our Kids students come up from Gilbert.
Is there a Women's-only class for Gilbert moms?
Yes. Our Women's Jiu-Jitsu class runs Tuesdays and Thursdays at 6:30 pm — popular with Gilbert moms whose kids are in the youth class at the same time.
Do you offer family rates?
Yes, we have multi-family-member discounts. Bring it up at your free trial and we'll get you set up.
