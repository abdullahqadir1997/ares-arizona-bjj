Chandler FAQs

What Chandler residents ask us.

How long is the drive from Chandler to Ares Arizona BJJ?
About 15 minutes up Alma School Rd. Coming from south Chandler / Ocotillo, expect closer to 20 minutes.
Do you have programs that work for Chandler families with kids and adults?
Yes. We run Kids Jiu-Jitsu (ages 4–14) and Adult BJJ classes back-to-back most evenings, so families can train together in the same trip.
Is there a class that fits a 9-to-5 schedule out of Chandler?
Our 6:00 pm Adult Gi class and 7:00 pm No-Gi block are the most popular for Chandler professionals leaving work in north Chandler.
Can I commute to morning class before work in Chandler?
Yes — our 6:00 am class wraps by 7:00 am, leaving plenty of time to head back south for an 8:30 or 9:00 am start.
