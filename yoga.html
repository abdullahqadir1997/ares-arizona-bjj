Yoga

Move better. Roll longer.

Mobility, recovery, and breath work designed for grapplers and martial artists. A weekly yoga practice will keep you on the mats — and off the injury list — for decades.

Why Yoga at a BJJ Gym

The longest careers in jiu-jitsu belong to the most flexible.

Every black belt who's been on the mats for 20+ years has one thing in common: they take care of their body. Our yoga program is built specifically for grapplers, martial artists, and active adults who want to stay active for the long haul.

Forget what you've seen on Instagram. This isn't about pretzeling yourself into impossible shapes. It's about rebuilding the hip, shoulder, and spinal mobility that decades of sitting at a desk have stolen. It's about teaching your nervous system how to actually relax. It's about giving you the breath capacity that turns a 30-minute roll from gasping survival into calm flow.

Whether you train jiu-jitsu and want to recover faster, or you've never set foot on a mat and just want to move better — our yoga classes are open to all and beginner friendly.

Why It Works

What you'll walk away with.

01

Hip Mobility for Guard

Open guard, closed guard, lasso, De La Riva — every guard system depends on hip mobility. A weekly yoga class is the single fastest way to unlock yours.

02

Faster Recovery

Active recovery beats passive recovery every time. A 60-minute yoga class flushes lactate, restores tissue length, and gets you back on the mats fresh.

03

Bulletproof Joints

Targeted shoulder, hip, knee, and spinal mobility work prevents the most common grappling injuries before they ever happen. Train smart, train forever.

04

Better Breath Control

Pranayama (breath work) is the secret weapon of every elite grappler. Higher cardio capacity. Faster recovery between rolls. The ability to stay calm under pressure.

05

Mental Reset

Sixty minutes of moving meditation. Your phone stays in the locker. Your nervous system finally exhales. You'll leave class lighter than you came in.

06

Functional Strength

Yoga builds the small-stabilizer strength that lifting weights ignores. The result: a body that moves better, feels better, and lasts longer.
