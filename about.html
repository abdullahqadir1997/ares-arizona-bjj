About Ares Arizona

Forged in Mesa. Built for everyone.

We're a family-owned Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu academy run by world champion black belt Paul Nava and his wife Leslie. We exist to give Mesa a place where serious technique and real community live under the same roof.

Our Story

A gym built on the oldest idea in martial arts: respect.

Ares Arizona Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu was founded with one mission — to create a world-class jiu-jitsu academy that doesn't sacrifice culture for ego. Coach Paul Nava has dedicated his life to the gentle art, earning his black belt under one of the most respected lineages in the sport and competing at the highest levels of the IBJJF circuit.

What you'll find on our mats isn't just technique. It's a family. Adults training next to teenagers. Parents rolling with their kids. White belts being coached by purple belts who remember exactly what their first month felt like. We are Ares — named after the Greek god of war — but we don't train for fights. We train for life.

We're a proud ESA Vendor approved by the Arizona Department of Education, and we offer dedicated discounts for military, first responders, and families. Because the people who serve and protect this community deserve a place to recover, decompress, and grow.
